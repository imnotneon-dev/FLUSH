What is Flush PH?

A forum built for toilets in the Philippines.

Flush PH is a community-driven forum where Filipinos can talk about anything and everything. Feel free to share your opinions, post images and converse with other users!

We named it Flush because good ideas, hot takes, and honest conversations shouldn't be held in. It should flow.

Objectives of Flush PH

Our goals and mission.

Restroom hygiene is a common concern, especially for those who frequently attend school, work, or visit public places. Aside from the discomfort in using unclean restrooms, they also pose serious health risks, as they can serve as breeding grounds for harmful and transmissible diseases.

Flush PH aims to solve this problem by providing a platform for users to review and discuss the quality of restrooms in the country. This allows users to choose the better restroom for a more comfortable experience, but it also holds those responsible for maintaining restrooms accountable.
